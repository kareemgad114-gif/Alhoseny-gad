Graduation Project

The capstone project for my degree — and the part of it I built.

🌿

Smart Farming

IoT precision-agriculture platform · Graduation project, 2026

Project grade: Excellent

My role: Flutter app

Smart Farming monitors a real farm through four field sensors — soil temperature, soil moisture, air temperature and air humidity — and turns those readings into decisions a farmer can act on. Sensor logs land in Firebase Firestore, and the app reads them live.

I built the Flutter mobile application: the full interface, the Firestore data layer, the live dashboard and charts, remote pump control, the alert system, and the screens that present the machine-learning output — a Random Forest model that recommends a crop and scores plant-disease risk. The app ships bilingual (Arabic / English) with a dark mode.
